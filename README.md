Boardify: An Android-Based Platform for Tenant-Landlord Management System
Project Description
Boardify is a comprehensive Android application designed to modernize the rental experience for landlords and tenants in Daet, Camarines Norte.

The primary goal of Boardify is to streamline the boarding house management process. It serves as a localized marketplace where landlords can post vacancies and a management tool for existing tenants. Once a tenant rents a room, they are automatically connected to their specific boarding house ecosystem within the app. This platform eliminates manual record-keeping by digitizing payments, maintenance requests, and communication.

Key Features
🏠 For Landlords
·Vacancy Posting: Easily publish listings for available boarding house rooms to attract future tenants in the Daet area.

·Automated Rent Management: Organize tenant lists and track payment statuses automatically.

·Due Date Notifications: Send automated or manual push notifications to tenants when rent is approaching or past due.

·Maintenance Oversight: Receive, view, and manage repair requests from tenants in a centralized dashboard.

👤 For Tenants
·In-App Payments: Securely pay monthly rent directly through the application.

·Maintenance Requests: File reports for necessary repairs (e.g., plumbing, electrical) with descriptions and photos.

·Community Group Chat: Access a dedicated group chat for the specific boarding house to communicate with the landlord and other housemates.

·Digital Onboarding: Automatically connect to the landlord's system upon renting a room
